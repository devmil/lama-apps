Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Lama apps family page at devmil.de/lama-apps handles personal data. The page introduces ProcessLama, MailLama, GitLama, ShotLama and NoteLama; it is a website, not an app.

Michael Lamers

2. Overview

This website is built to collect as little as possible. Devmil Solutions does not run analytics, advertising or tracking. The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third-party servers: its styles, scripts and images come from devmil.de, and text is set in the fonts already installed on your device. Links to other websites, such as the apps’ own pages or GitHub, only take you there when you follow them.

3. Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the site is visited, GitHub processes technical access data to deliver it and keep it secure: IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer, browser and operating system. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website).

GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub privacy statement. Devmil Solutions has no access to these logs.

4. Local storage in your browser

The website stores the language you choose (preferred-language) in your browser’s local storage. It stores nothing else. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted, and can be deleted in your browser at any time. Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for a service you requested).

5. The apps

This page is the family page of the Lama apps. It is not an app and exchanges no data with any of them. Each app has its own privacy policy, which explains what the app does with your data:

  • ProcessLama Process explorer
  • MailLama Desktop client for Proton Mail
  • GitLama Git client
  • ShotLama Screenshot and screen recording studio
  • NoteLama Handwritten notebooks

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, www.lda.bayern.de.

To exercise your rights or ask about privacy, contact info [at] devmil [dot] de.

7. Changes

This policy is updated when the website changes. The current version is always available on this page.
